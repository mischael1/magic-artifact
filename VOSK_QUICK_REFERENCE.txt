APP FLOW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

User Press "Start"
    ↓
VoiceManager.startListening()
    ↓
AudioRecord captures microphone
    ↓
Vosk Recognizer processes audio
    ↓
Partial results → UI updates in real-time
Final result → SpellRecognizer matches spell
    ↓
UI shows spell name & description (or "not found")

User Press "Stop"
    ↓
VoiceManager.stopListening()
    ↓
Resources cleaned up

Problem: "Cannot find vosk-android-0.3.50.aar"

Problem: No audio capture
→ Check permission in app settings
→ Physical device works better than emulator

Problem: No results after speaking
→ Check logs: adb logcat | grep VoiceManager
→ Verify microphone is working
→ Try clear app data and reinstall
